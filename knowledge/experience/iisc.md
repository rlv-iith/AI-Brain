# Research Intern — Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore

**Duration:** Dec 2025 – Apr 2026  
**Lab:** Single Molecular Science Lab  
**Type:** Academic research internship

## Research focus
Molecular electronics — measuring electrical and thermoelectric properties of single-molecule junctions. This is frontier research at the boundary of quantum chemistry and nanotechnology.

## Lalith's contributions
- Designed and built a new experimental setup to measure **thermopower** (Seebeck coefficient) in molecular junctions — a quantity that tells you how efficiently a molecule converts heat to electricity
- Built hardware systems for precise **temperature control** during measurements (millikelvin stability)
- Wrote Python code to interface with lab instruments (lock-in amplifiers, temperature controllers) and automate data collection pipelines
- Used **SEM (Scanning Electron Microscopy)** to characterise fabricated nano-gap devices

## Skills demonstrated
Experimental physics, Python for instrument control, data acquisition, scientific computing, cross-disciplinary collaboration with PhD researchers.
